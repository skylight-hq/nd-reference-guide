How to use this guide

This is a reference guide to use while you work through a case. It is not part of the eligibility system and does not make the decision for you.

  1. Start with application or review. The guide figures out the lookback period.
  2. Answer one step at a time. When a step gives a result, you can stop there.
  3. The narrative on the right writes itself as you go. Copy it and paste it into the eligibility system.
  4. Click Start over before the next case.

Nothing you enter is saved or sent. Close the page and it's gone. Don’t enter names or case numbers.

Narrative

Updates as you answer. Copy and paste into the system narrative. Add your own notes in each step.
